Measures of Association

One group gives you a measure of occurrence. Two groups give you a comparison — a difference or a ratio — and that comparison is what every abstract reports. Five stations on the four numbers that do most of the work (risk difference, risk ratio, rate ratio, odds ratio), which study window can produce which, and what none of them can say. If “risk” and “rate” still feel loose, run Measures of Occurrence first.

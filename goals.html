EBM Through Blind Spots — the learning goals, as a map

Every learning goal of the course on one connected map. Solid links grow a skill inside one part of the course (Knowledge → Application → Creation); dashed links are bridges between parts. The Roman numeral on a goal is the week that teaches it; weeks VIII and XIV are the controls, where goals are not taught but graded. Click any goal or link to read what it asks and how it is checked — goals marked ⚡ carry the course trainer or a reading. Pick a trail to follow one course-level outcome across the whole map. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, drag goals to untangle.
